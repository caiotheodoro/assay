| Slice 32b | Whose assumption actually failed. | `src/assay/probes/verifier.py` | **Assay's.** The verifier-integrity family takes for granted that a verifier separates correct from incorrect. On preference elicitation, opinion surveys and psychometric inventories that is false, and four of twelve probes become *actively misleading* rather than merely inapplicable. The distinction matters: a probe reporting `NOT_APPLICABLE` costs a reader nothing, while **a probe reporting CRITICAL on a healthy environment costs the tool its credibility**, and no amount of NOT_APPLICABLE discipline elsewhere protects against it. The right fix is a capability an eval can withhold — "this environment has no correct answer" — and it does not exist. |
